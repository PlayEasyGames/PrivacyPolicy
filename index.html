Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to all games and applications developed and published by PlayEasy Games ("PlayEasy Games", "we", "us", or "our"), including games released in the future under the PlayEasy Games brand.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use our games and applications (collectively, the "Games"), how we use and share that information, and the choices available to you.

The specific information collected, used, or shared may vary depending on the particular Game, features enabled, platform, and third-party services used by that Game. Certain sections of this Privacy Policy may therefore apply only to Games that offer the corresponding features or use the corresponding services.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the Game and the features you use, we may collect or receive the following types of information.

1.1 Device and Technical Information

We may collect technical information about the device and environment used to access our Games, including:

  • Device model and manufacturer
  • Operating system and version
  • App version
  • Language and regional settings
  • Time zone
  • IP address or approximate location derived from IP address
  • Advertising identifiers and similar device identifiers, where permitted
  • Diagnostic and technical information

This information may be collected automatically by PlayEasy Games or by third-party services integrated into our Games.

1.2 Gameplay and Game Data

We may collect or store information related to your use of a Game, such as:

  • Game progress
  • Completed levels
  • Scores and high scores
  • Achievements
  • Leaderboard information
  • Daily challenge progress
  • Game settings and preferences
  • Virtual items or other game-related information

Some game data may be stored locally on your device. Where a Game supports account-based or cloud-save features, relevant data may also be stored using our servers or third-party cloud services.

1.3 Account and Authentication Information

Some Games may allow you to play as a guest or sign in using a third-party authentication service.

If you use an authentication service such as Google Sign-In, we may receive information made available to us by that service, such as:

  • A unique account or user identifier
  • Basic account or profile information made available by the authentication provider
  • Information necessary to associate your account with your Game progress

Guest accounts may use a randomly generated identifier to associate your Game progress with a particular game account.

We do not receive or store your Google account password.

1.4 Analytics and Diagnostic Information

We may collect information about how you use our Games, including:

  • Sessions and session duration
  • Levels played or completed
  • Features used
  • In-game events
  • General gameplay activity
  • Crash reports
  • Error information
  • Performance and diagnostic information

We use this information to understand how our Games are used, identify technical problems, improve performance, and improve the Games.

1.5 Advertising Information

If a Game contains advertising, advertising providers or mediation platforms may collect information necessary to display, measure, and personalize advertisements, subject to applicable laws and your device or platform settings.

This may include advertising identifiers, device information, approximate location, ad interaction information, and other information permitted by the applicable advertising provider.

Advertising practices may vary between Games.

1.6 In-App Purchases

If a Game offers in-app purchases, purchases are processed through the applicable platform, such as Google Play or the Apple App Store.

We may receive information about a purchase, such as:

  • Product purchased
  • Purchase status
  • Transaction or purchase identifier
  • Date or other transaction information necessary to provide the purchased content

We do not receive or store your full payment card, bank account, or other payment credentials used to complete purchases through the applicable app store.

1.7 Support and Communications

If you contact us for support or other inquiries, we may collect the information you voluntarily provide, such as your email address, device information, Game information, and the contents of your communication.

2. How We Use Information

We may use information collected through our Games to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain our Games
  • Save and restore Game progress
  • Provide cloud-save and account features
  • Provide leaderboards, achievements, and other online features
  • Authenticate users and manage accounts
  • Process and verify in-app purchases
  • Display and measure advertising
  • Provide personalized advertising where permitted
  • Analyze Game usage and engagement
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, or unauthorized activity
  • Diagnose crashes, errors, and technical problems
  • Improve Game performance, functionality, and user experience
  • Respond to support requests
  • Communicate important service-related information
  • Comply with applicable laws and legal obligations

3. Third-Party Services

Our Games may use third-party services to provide functionality such as authentication, analytics, crash reporting, advertising, cloud storage, leaderboards, and payment processing.

Depending on the Game, these services may include:

  • Google Firebase, including Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Authentication, and related services
  • Google Sign-In
  • Google Play Games Services
  • Google AdMob
  • Unity Ads and/or Unity LevelPlay
  • Apple Game Center
  • Google Play
  • Apple App Store

The services used by a particular Game may differ.

Third-party service providers may collect and process information in accordance with their own privacy policies and terms. We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of the services used by the Game you are playing.

We may add, remove, or change third-party service providers as our Games and services evolve.

4. Advertising

Some of our Games may display advertisements.

Advertising may be provided directly by advertising networks or through advertising mediation platforms. These providers may collect information about your device, advertising identifier, approximate location, ad interactions, and other information permitted under applicable law to provide, measure, and personalize advertising.

Where required by applicable law, we will request appropriate consent before using information for personalized advertising.

You may also be able to control advertising personalization through your device or platform settings.

5. Leaderboards and Online Features

Some Games may provide leaderboards, competitions, events, achievements, or similar online features.

If you participate in these features, information such as your Game username, player identifier, score, ranking, achievements, or other relevant Game data may be processed and, where applicable, displayed to other players.

The information displayed depends on the specific Game and feature.

6. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information for money.

We may share information with third parties in the following circumstances:

Service Providers

We may share information with third-party providers that help us operate our Games, including providers of:

  • Authentication
  • Cloud storage and infrastructure
  • Analytics
  • Crash reporting
  • Advertising
  • Payment and purchase processing
  • Customer support
  • Security and fraud prevention

These providers process information on our behalf or as otherwise described in their applicable privacy policies.

App Stores and Platforms

Information may be shared with or received from platforms such as Google Play and the Apple App Store as necessary to distribute our Games, process purchases, provide platform features, and comply with platform requirements.

Legal Requirements

We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to:

  • Comply with applicable laws or legal processes
  • Respond to lawful requests from government authorities
  • Protect our rights, property, or safety
  • Investigate fraud, abuse, or security issues
  • Enforce our terms or policies

Business Transfers

If PlayEasy Games or substantially all of its assets are involved in a merger, acquisition, reorganization, financing, or sale, information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to applicable law.

7. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our Games, maintain accounts and services, fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

The retention period may vary depending on the type of information and the Game.

Some information may remain in backup systems for a limited period after deletion where reasonably necessary for security, legal, or operational purposes.

8. Children's Privacy

Our Games may have different intended audiences.

Unless a Game is specifically identified as being directed toward children, our Games are intended for a general audience and are not directed toward children below the applicable minimum age under local law.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable privacy laws.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us in circumstances where such collection was not permitted, please contact us. We will take appropriate steps to investigate and, where required, delete the information.

For Games that are specifically directed toward children, additional privacy practices and parental consent requirements may apply as required by applicable law.

9. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information.

These may include the right to:

  • Request access to personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Request restriction of certain processing
  • Object to certain processing
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
  • Request information about how your personal information is processed

The availability of these rights depends on applicable law and your location.

To exercise applicable rights, contact us using the information provided in Section 13.

Advertising Choices

Depending on your device and location, you may be able to control advertising personalization through your device settings or through consent options presented within a Game.

Notifications

If a Game uses push notifications, you can generally disable them through your device settings.

10. Account and Data Deletion

If a Game provides account-based features, you may request deletion of your account and associated personal information by contacting us.

When processing a deletion request, we may need information reasonably necessary to verify the request and identify the relevant account.

Some information may be retained where required or permitted by law, or where reasonably necessary for legitimate purposes such as fraud prevention, security, dispute resolution, or financial recordkeeping.

Deleting an account or Game data may result in the loss of Game progress, purchases, achievements, leaderboard information, or other associated content where such data cannot be retained or restored separately.

11. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure. Therefore, we cannot guarantee absolute security of information.

12. International Data Transfers

Depending on the services used by a Game, information may be processed or stored in countries other than the country in which you live.

Third-party service providers may operate infrastructure in multiple countries.

Where required by applicable law, we take or require appropriate safeguards for international transfers of personal information.
